# Lore Challenge — verification record for the questions added in the 150 → 300 expansion

Scope: the 150 questions numbered 11–20 in each category and difficulty. The original 150 (01–10) are not covered here.

## How to read this record

- **Checked online**: the fact was confirmed in this session against the page listed (secondary source: Tolkien Gateway). The primary work is named only at book/appendix level; no page or chapter numbers are claimed.
- **Not checked online**: written from the published works (The Hobbit, The Lord of the Rings and its Appendices, The Silmarillion) and **not re-confirmed against a source in this session**. These need an editorial pass against the books before the content is treated as verified.
- No passages from the books are reproduced; questions and explanations are in the project's own words.

Known limitation found during checking: the Enerdhil/Elessar attribution was dropped because Tolkien's sources disagree (some name Celebrimbor), so it is not used.

## Fourth pass (browser verification against Tolkien Gateway, questions 11-20)
Scope: exactly the 150 questions numbered 11-20 (5 categories x 3 difficulties x 10). `history-hard-05` (added in the third pass) and questions 01-10 are outside this scope; the earlier records and passes above are kept unchanged.

### Method and honest limits

- **Access.** Tolkien Gateway (tolkiengateway.net) was reachable through the Chrome browser extension, with the user's explicit authorisation. No proxy, mirror or bypass was used. The earlier automated fetcher had received HTTP 403; the browser did not.
- **What was actually read.** For each question the specific Tolkien Gateway page for the fact was loaded by the browser and the sentences that matched the terms of the question were extracted and read. The page `Peregrin_Took` was read in full; every other page was read **only at snippet level** (the best-matching sentences), not end to end. A row never claims more than the snippets showed; what a snippet did not show is listed as 'not in the snippet read'.
- **Secondary source.** Tolkien Gateway is a fan-maintained encyclopedia. These rows do **not** mean that the passage was checked directly in the books. The Tolkien Gateway pages themselves cite the books, but those passages were not consulted here.
- **Earlier records are kept.** The Wikipedia / Encyclopedia of Arda results of pass 3 stay in the tables above. This pass adds a separate, independent source; where the two differ, the row below says what the browser pass saw.
- **Books vs films.** Several Tolkien Gateway pages mix book and adaptation material. Only the book-level statements were used; film-only statements are noted as such.

### Classification used

- **SUPPORTED**: the answer and the facts that define the question were found on the specific page, and nothing in the explanation was contradicted. Peripheral explanation details that the snippets did not show are listed in the issue column but do not lower the class.
- **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED**: the core answer is right, but a material clue or explanation claim was not confirmed in this pass or is imprecise.
- **UNSUPPORTED** / **NOT VERIFIED**: none were needed; every one of the 150 pages loaded and gave support for the answer.
