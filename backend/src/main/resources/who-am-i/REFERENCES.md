# Who Am I? — sources and verification record

Scope: the 36 characters (12 per difficulty) and their 180 clues in `characters.json`.

## How to read this record

All sources are **Tolkien Gateway** pages (a secondary, community-written encyclopedia). The primary works
(The Hobbit, The Lord of the Rings and its Appendices, The Silmarillion) were **not** consulted directly,
and no page or chapter numbers are claimed. Where a Tolkien Gateway page cites a chapter, that citation is
the wiki's, not something confirmed against the books here. Wikipedia was not used as a source.

On **2026-11-05** every page listed under a character was opened (HTTP 200) and checked against each clue.
On **2026-11-06** an editorial audit re-read the passages behind the 146 clues that had only been checked by
key terms, and the remaining open details were checked against further Tolkien Gateway pages (chapter,
object and place pages, listed in the Source column). The status of each clue is one of:

- **Passage read**: the passage that supports the clue was read on the cited page and the clue is worded to
  match it. This is a check against a secondary source only.
- **Partly confirmed (see audit)**: the central fact is on the page, but one detail of the wording is not;
  the detail is listed in the audit notes below.

No clue is marked as verified against the primary works.

## Changes made because a page did not confirm a first draft

These claims were in the first draft of the clues and were reworded or replaced, because the page did not
state them (the facts may well be true in the books, but they were not confirmed, so they are not used):

- Frodo's parents "drowned in a river": the page says they died in a boating accident.
- Gandalf and "the pipe-weed of the Shire": the page says only that he was often seen with a pipe.
- Boromir's horn "heard across long distances": the page describes a great horn tipped with silver.
- Éowyn's brother "banished": the page says Éomer was arrested and imprisoned on Gríma's orders.
- Théoden "rode out at dawn" at Helm's Deep: the page says he won the Battle of the Hornburg and was named the Renewed.
- Thorin "asked forgiveness": the page says he apologized to Bilbo.
- Pippin "wore black and silver": the page says he pledged allegiance and became a Guard of the Citadel.
- Fingolfin's foe "limped" and "fell beneath a hammer": the page says the wounds never fully healed and Morgoth killed him; his body was taken to a mountaintop by Thorondor.
- Lúthien's "cloak of hair": not on the page; replaced by the imprisonment in the beech house, which the Hírilorn page states.
- Gimli's Orc-tally with Legolas: the page says they slew many orcs; replaced by his sailing West, which it states.
- Elrond "fostered by two sons of Fëanor": the page names Maglor as foster father; Maedhros is named as one of the captors.
- Ungoliant "came out of the Void": the page says her origins are a mystery, with that idea only in an early version; reworded.

## Editorial audit (2026-11-06)

Clues reworded because the sources did not support the original wording: Samwise 3 (Galadriel is a lady,
not a queen; the page says "orchard"), Bilbo 3 (he found the Ring before the riddle game), Saruman 5 (an
assumed name, not a disguise), Thorin 2 (the thirteen Dwarves include Thorin), Beorn 2 (a man who takes
bear shape; no "by day/by night"), Thingol 2 (the Girdle is Melian's work), Lúthien 3 (spell and dance,
not song), Ungoliant 3 (the Trees withered; no poison).

Details checked against further secondary pages. No primary text was reachable in a form that could be
read in full, so none of these is a primary verification:

- Reworded after the check: Frodo 3 (the page says the wound ached a year later; it does not say it never
  healed, and says Elrond healed it), Gandalf 1 (the Istari were to advise and persuade; "rather than rule"
  not found), Éowyn 5 (the page gives only that her longing for war faded and she wed Faramir), Treebeard 4
  (the Entmoot "lasted for days"; "three days" not stated), Tom Bombadil 2 (Goldberry is called the
  River-daughter; her parentage is uncertain on the page), Gríma 2 (Saruman promised him Éowyn; removing
  others not found), Beorn 1 (the page says horses, not cattle), Túrin 4 (the page says Túrin approached
  from below; "crawling" not found).
- Kept: Aragorn 3 (Strider offers guidance and leads the hobbits out of Bree), Gimli 3 (the chapter page
  speaks of many days of pursuit), Sauron 5 ("Lidless Eye" is on the Eye of Sauron page), Treebeard 5
  (the Battle of Isengard page says the dam was broken and Isengard flooded), Túrin 2 (Húrin refused
  Morgoth and was cursed with his kin), Túrin 3 (Gurthang answers in a cold voice), Thingol 5 (Melian
  departed on Thingol's death and the Girdle vanished).
- Source corrected: Sauron 4 (the broken sword is on the Elendil page) and Beren 2 (the ring is on the
  Finrod page).
- Note: the Girdle of Melian page says the enchantment kept out anyone without "her or King Thingol's
  consent"; Thingol 2 credits Melian and does not mention consent.

Details still not on the cited page (clues kept unchanged): Galadriel 3 ("things that are" in the Mirror),
Galadriel 4 ("terrible queen"), Saruman 2 ("black", "ringed"), Gríma 4 ("high window", "nearly striking"),
Eärendil 5 ("at dawn"), Peregrin 1 ("spoke before thinking" is an interpretation).
