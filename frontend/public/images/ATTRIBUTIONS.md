# Image credits and provenance

Wunderlore is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project (see the notice in the site footer).

## What is and is not known

The photographs and artwork used as scene backgrounds were downloaded by the project owner from
image platforms (Freepik, Pexels, Pixabay and Unsplash), in the modes those platforms offer for free
use. That is **all** that is known about their origin.

For these images, the project did not record:

- the author or photographer;
- the original page, or the image ID;
- the individual licence text in force on the day of download;
- whether that licence asks for a credit.

Nothing below fills those gaps with guesses. This list does not claim that each image has a
confirmed, individual credit, and it is **not** proof that a licence is valid or that no obligation
applies. Anyone who recognises one of these images and can document its author or licence is welcome
to tell the project owner so that the entry can be corrected, or the image replaced.

The site never changes the photographs beyond the processing described in the table. It darkens them
with a green overlay on the page.

## Artwork made for the project

- **Character symbols** (Which Character Are You?): ten inline SVG drawings made for this project: a
  ring on a chain (Frodo), a seedling (Sam), a sword (Aragorn), a staff (Gandalf), a burning eye
  (Sauron), a fish (Gollum), a bow and arrow (Legolas), an axe (Gimli), a star above two leaves
  (Galadriel) and a horn (Boromir). No third-party artwork was traced, copied or adapted, and no film
  costume, likeness or promotional art was used.
- **Site icon** (`icon.svg`, a leaf with a ring): a simple vector drawing made for the project.
  `favicon.ico` is the fallback icon; its creation is not documented here.

The code and these drawings follow the project licence (see the repository `LICENSE`).

licensees; the film adaptations belong to their rights holders. Wunderlore is not affiliated with,
endorsed or sponsored by any of them. Whether a fan project like this is acceptable outside a
