## Character symbols (Which Character Are You?)

The ten symbols are inline SVG drawn for this project in
`src/app/games/which-character/characterSymbols.tsx`: a ring on a chain (Frodo), a seedling (Sam),
a sword (Aragorn), a staff (Gandalf), a burning eye (Sauron), a fish (Gollum), a bow and arrow
(Legolas), an axe (Gimli), a star above two leaves (Galadriel) and a horn (Boromir).

- **Creator:** the Wunderlore project. No third-party artwork was traced, copied or adapted, and no
  film costume, likeness or promotional art was used.
- **License:** same as the project (see the repository `LICENSE`). Attribution: none required.
- **Third-party rights:** the characters and their world belong to the Tolkien Estate and its
  licensees. The symbols are unofficial fan work for a non-commercial portfolio, with no endorsement.
  Whether that is acceptable beyond a portfolio is a rights question for the project owner.
